UNCHARTED

AI built on Meta's Muse models has beaten 5 world records in mathematics.

We built an AI research team. It invents its own methods, writes the code, tests it, studies pictures of its results, and keeps going until it finds a better answer than the best one mathematicians have ever published.

The story in 30 seconds

1
The challenge

Spread a number of points over a sphere so that no two are close together. For 150+ points in four dimensions, nobody knows the best arrangement. Mathematicians keep a public table of the best answers found so far.

2
The AI writes code

Muse Spark invents a method and writes a program to search for a better arrangement. Eight attempts run at once.

3
It checks, then looks

Each program runs in a safe sandbox and a strict checker scores the answer. The AI then studies a picture of the result to see what to fix next.

4
A lead AI steers

Muse Wonder reviews all attempts every few rounds, works out why progress stalled, and assigns new strategies.

5
A new world record

When an answer beats the published best, we recheck it independently with 50-digit arithmetic.

Why it matters: this geometry decides how well signals can be told apart, so it sits underneath radio, Wi-Fi and error-correcting codes. And it shows AI doing real research: finding results nobody had found before.
